01 — Data flow

Data moves one way.

Select a step to see what passes through it. The return path does not exist: Nonius has no endpoint that sends instructions to your bank.

02 — What we store

What we keep, and what we never see.

The ledger holds what is needed to calculate your figures. Everything that could move money stays with your bank.

Stored in your ledger

  • Account names, balances and positions
  • Transactions: date, type, quantity, price, fees, taxes
  • Assets and values you enter by hand
  • Settings, alerts and your base currency
  • Email address and sign-in factors: a password hash and passkey public keys

Never stored

  • Passwords for your bank or broker
  • Card numbers: subscription payments go straight to the payment provider
  • Any permission to place orders or initiate payments
  • Location, contacts or advertising identifiers
  • Copies of identity documents

03 — Where and how

Stored in the EU. Encrypted at rest and on the wire.

Hosting
FRA · AMSEU data centres in Frankfurt and Amsterdam. Your data is stored in the EU and nowhere else.
At rest
AES-256Databases, backups and export files are encrypted with AES-256.
In transit
TLS 1.3Every connection, to your browser and to account providers, uses TLS 1.3.

04 — Access

You hold the keys.

Revoke a connection

  1. 1Open Settings → Linked accounts.
  2. 2Choose the account and select Revoke access.
  3. 3Nonius deletes the token at once. The account stops syncing; its history stays in your ledger until you delete it.

You can also withdraw access at your bank. Nonius then marks the account as disconnected at the next sync.

Two-factor sign-in

  • Authenticator apps (TOTP)Six-digit codes from any standard authenticator app.
  • PasskeysSign in with your device’s fingerprint, face or PIN; the private key never leaves the device.
  • Recovery codesSingle-use codes, shown once, for the day you lose the device.

05 — What we never do

Three things Nonius will not do.

  • Trade

    No orders, no transfers, no payments. The permission does not exist, and neither does the code.

  • Sell your data

    Your ledger is not sold, rented or shared with advertisers or data brokers. Subscriptions are the only income.

  • Advise

    Nonius measures what happened. It does not tell you what to buy or sell, and it is not a licensed adviser.

07 — Incidents

If something goes wrong, you hear it from us.

If a breach affects your data, we report it to the Dutch Data Protection Authority within 72 hours, as the GDPR requires, and email you directly with what happened and what to do next.

Report a vulnerability

Write to security@nonius.example. We confirm receipt within one business day and take no legal action against research done in good faith.
