03 — Billing questions

Billing, in plain terms.

What happens when the trial ends?+

Your account moves to Free unless you have chosen Plus or Pro. Nothing is deleted: connections above the free limit pause, and history older than 12 months is hidden until you upgrade.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?+

Yes, in Settings → Billing. A move to yearly billing starts at once and the unused part of the current month is credited. A move to monthly billing takes effect when the paid year ends.

What if I downgrade with more accounts than Free allows?+

You choose which 2 connections keep syncing. The others stop updating but stay in your ledger with their last balances, so totals and history remain readable.

How can I pay?+

By card or SEPA Direct Debit, in euros. Every payment comes with a PDF invoice in Settings → Billing.

Can I cancel, and do I get a refund?+

You can cancel at any time; access continues until the end of the period you paid for. Within 14 days of your first payment you can ask for a full refund, no questions asked.

Is the free plan paid for with my data?+

No. Subscriptions are the only income. Nonius does not sell or share data, show advertising or take commissions from brokers.
