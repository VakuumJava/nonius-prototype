Account

Which languages and currencies does Nonius support?+

The interface is available in English, German and Russian; switch it from the language menu in the header. Your base currency can be EUR, USD, GBP or CHF, and you can change it at any time in Settings. Holdings in other currencies, such as Danish kroner, are converted into the base currency at the daily rate.

Does Nonius work on my phone?+

Yes. Nonius runs in the browser on desktop, tablet and phone, in light and dark themes. There is nothing to install: sign in from any browser and your accounts and ledger are there. Display preferences such as the colour theme are remembered per browser.

How do I delete my account?+

Open Settings, then Account, and choose Delete account. Every connection token is revoked at that moment and your ledger is removed from our live systems straight away; backup copies are overwritten within 35 days. If you want to keep a copy, export your data first.

Connections & data

Can Nonius move money or place trades?+

No. Every connection uses read-only access: Nonius can read balances and transactions, and nothing else. You can revoke a connection in Settings at any time, and its token stops working immediately.

Which kinds of accounts can I add?+

Brokerage accounts, bank accounts, pensions, crypto wallets and manual assets such as property or a private loan. There are three ways in: a read-only connection, a CSV import of your broker’s export, or manual entry.

How often are balances updated?+

Connected accounts usually refresh every few hours, depending on the institution. If a connection stops syncing, the account is marked as needing attention and a banner shows when it last updated and how to reconnect.

My broker is not on the list. What can I do?+

Upload the CSV export your broker already produces. Nonius recognises common column layouts and shows a preview before anything is saved, so you can correct a column first. You can also enter positions by hand.

Can I export my data?+

Yes. Every table can be exported as CSV, and the performance report as PDF. The CSV export contains your full transaction ledger, so you can take your data elsewhere at any time.

Analytics

Which return figure does Nonius show?+

The headline figure is the time-weighted return: deposits and withdrawals are taken out, so it shows how the investments themselves performed. Analytics also shows the money-weighted return, which reflects when you added or took out money. The journal explains both with a worked example.

How is the currency effect worked out?+

Every position is converted into your base currency at each day’s rate. The change in value is then split into the price move in the instrument’s own currency and the currency move, and both are reported separately, per position and in total.

Are dividends shown before or after tax?+

After withholding tax, as they arrive in your account. Nonius records what was paid; it does not calculate your tax or reclaim anything on your behalf. For your own situation, ask a tax adviser.

Plans & billing

What do the plans cost?+

Free costs nothing and covers 2 linked accounts and 12 months of history. Plus costs €8 a month, or €72 a year (€6 a month). Pro costs €18 a month, or €168 a year (€14 a month). Plus and Pro both remove the account limit and keep your full history.

How does the free trial work?+

Paid plans start with a 30-day trial. If you do not choose a plan by the end of it, the account moves to the free plan. Nothing is deleted: connections beyond the free limit pause until you upgrade or remove some.

Can I cancel at any time?+

Yes, from Settings. The plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for; after that the account moves to the free plan and your data stays as it is.

Security

Where is my data stored, and how is it protected?+

In EU data centres in Frankfurt and Amsterdam. Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Connection tokens are read-only, so even a leaked token cannot move money.

Does Nonius support two-factor sign-in?+

Yes. You can use an authenticator app that generates one-time codes (TOTP) or a passkey. Turn it on in Settings, under Security; we recommend doing so before you connect your first account.
