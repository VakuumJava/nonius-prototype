Privacy notice

This notice explains which personal data Nonius processes, why, and what control you have over it. The short version: we read your financial data to show it to you, and for nothing else.

Nonius is a concept prototype. This text was written to show how the page works; it is not a contract, has not been reviewed by a lawyer, and no service is offered under it.

01Who is responsible

Nonius Technologies B.V., Amsterdam, the Netherlands, is the controller of the personal data described in this notice. Send questions about privacy to support@nonius.example and put “Privacy” in the subject line.

We answer requests about personal data within one month, as the GDPR requires.

02What we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address, language, base currency, plan and security settings.
  • Connection tokens: the read-only access tokens issued by your institutions. They allow balances and transactions to be read, never money to be moved.
  • Financial data: balances, holdings, transactions and income from the accounts you connect, the CSV files you import and the values you enter by hand.
  • Billing data: invoices and the payment status of paid plans. Card details are handled by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Diagnostics: technical logs such as error reports, browser type and the time of requests, which we need to run and secure the service.

03What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not build advertising profiles and we do not show ads.
  • We do not trade, place orders or move money on your behalf; our access is read-only.
  • We do not use your financial data to offer you products from third parties.

04Why we process data, and on what basis

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), every use of personal data needs a legal basis. Ours are:

  • Contract: to run your account, sync your connections, calculate returns and send service emails.
  • Legitimate interest: to keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fix errors, using no more data than needed.
  • Legal obligation: to keep invoices and to answer lawful requests from authorities.
  • Consent: for anything optional, such as product news by email. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the rest of your account.

05Where data is stored and how it is protected

Your data is stored in EU data centres in Frankfurt and Amsterdam. We do not transfer it outside the European Economic Area in normal operation.

Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3. Staff access is limited to the people who need it to run the service, and every access is logged.

You can protect your account further with two-factor authentication, using a TOTP app or a passkey.

06Service providers who process data for us

We work with a small number of processors, each bound by a written data processing agreement and each used only for the task described:

  • hosting and storage in the EU data centres named above;
  • email delivery for sign-in links, alerts and service messages;
  • payment processing for paid plans;
  • connection providers that relay read-only data from the institutions you choose to connect.

A current list of processors is available from support on request.

07How long we keep data

We keep account and financial data for as long as your account is open.

When you delete your account, your data is removed from our live systems immediately and every connection token is revoked. Backup copies are overwritten within 35 days.

Invoices are kept for as long as tax law requires, which in the Netherlands is currently seven years. Diagnostic logs are deleted after 90 days.

08Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data erased, which deleting your account does in one step;
  • receive your data in a portable format; the CSV export in the product covers your financial data;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest, and have processing restricted in certain cases.

To use these rights, write to support. If you believe we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Dutch Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens or to the supervisory authority in your own country.

09Cookies and local storage

Nonius uses only strictly necessary cookies and storage, so it does not ask for cookie consent:

  • a session cookie that keeps you signed in;
  • the nonius_locale cookie, which remembers your language;
  • your colour theme and display preferences, stored locally in your browser.

We do not use third-party trackers, analytics cookies or advertising pixels.

10Changes to this notice

When we change this notice, we publish the new version here with a new date and version number. If a change significantly affects how we use your data, we tell you by email before it applies.
