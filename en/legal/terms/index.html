01Who we are

Nonius is provided by Nonius Technologies B.V., a private limited company based in Amsterdam, the Netherlands. In these terms, “we” means Nonius Technologies B.V. and “you” the person who opens an account.

You can reach us at support@nonius.example, Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 CET; we reply within one business day.

02What Nonius is, and what it is not

Nonius is a portfolio tracker. It collects balances and transactions from the accounts you connect or import and shows them in one ledger, with returns, currency effects and income worked out.

Nonius is read-only. It cannot place orders, transfer funds or change anything at your bank, broker or wallet. Every connection uses a read-only access token.

Nonius does not give financial, investment or tax advice. Figures and charts in the product describe the past; they are not recommendations. For advice, speak to a qualified adviser.

03Your account

To open an account you must be at least 18 and able to enter into a contract. One account is for one person; do not share your login.

Keep your password safe and turn on two-factor authentication. Nonius supports TOTP authenticator apps and passkeys. If you think someone else has used your account, tell us straight away.

04Plans, trial and billing

Nonius has three plans:

  • Free: €0, up to 2 linked accounts and 12 months of history.
  • Plus: €8 a month or €72 a year, unlimited linked accounts and full history.
  • Pro: €18 a month or €168 a year, unlimited linked accounts and full history.

Paid plans start with a 30-day trial. If you have not chosen a plan when it ends, the account moves to the Free plan; nothing is charged unless you choose a paid plan, and we remind you by email a few days before the trial ends.

You can cancel at any time in Settings. Your plan runs until the end of the paid period and then moves to Free. Connections beyond the Free limit are paused, not deleted, and your full history is kept, even though Free shows only the last 12 months.

We announce price changes at least 30 days before they apply to you.

05Connections and data from third parties

When you connect an account, the data comes from your bank, broker, pension provider or wallet, sometimes through a licensed connection provider. We show what they send, but cannot guarantee that it is complete, correct or up to date.

Connected accounts usually sync every few hours. If an institution stops sending data, Nonius marks the account as out of date until you renew the connection. Check important figures against the institution’s own statement.

06Acceptable use

Use Nonius only for finances you own or are authorised to manage. Do not:

  • try to get into other people’s accounts or into our systems;
  • scrape, copy or resell the service or its market data;
  • upload files that contain malware, or data you have no right to share;
  • use Nonius in a way that breaks the law or puts other users at risk.

If an account breaks these rules, we may suspend it; where we can, we tell you first and explain why.

07Your content and exporting it

The data you connect, import or enter remains yours. You allow us to store and process it only to run Nonius for you, as described in the privacy notice.

You can export your data at any time: every table in the product as a CSV file, and your performance as a PDF report.

08Availability and changes to the service

We aim to keep Nonius available around the clock but do not promise uninterrupted service: maintenance, problems at institutions or hosting outages can interrupt it.

We develop Nonius continuously and may add, change or remove features. If we remove something important that you pay for, we tell you in advance, and if you cancel we refund the unused part of the paid period.

09Liability

Nonius shows information; it does not manage your money. We are not liable for investment decisions based on what you see, or for errors in data supplied by institutions and market data sources.

Where we are liable, our total liability is limited to the amount you paid us in the 12 months before the claim. On the Free plan, the limit is €100.

Nothing in these terms limits liability that the law does not allow to be limited, such as for intent or gross negligence, or reduces your rights as a consumer.

10Ending the agreement and deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. Deletion revokes every connection token at once and removes your data as described in the privacy notice. Export first if you want to keep a copy.

We may end the agreement with 30 days’ notice, or immediately if you seriously break these terms. If we end a paid plan without a reason on your side, we refund the unused part.

11Governing law and changes to these terms

These terms are governed by Dutch law. Disputes go to the competent court in Amsterdam, unless the law of your country of residence lets you go to a court there.

Changed terms are published here with a new date and version number. We announce changes that matter to you by email at least 30 days in advance; if you do not agree, you can delete your account before they apply.
