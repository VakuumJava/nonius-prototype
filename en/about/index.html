01 — Why it exists

Four statements, and no total.

A typical European investor holds an ETF savings plan at one broker, a few shares at another, a workplace pension and some crypto on the side. Each provider reports what it holds and nothing else.

Adding it up means a spreadsheet, exchange rates looked up by hand, and a return figure that mixes deposits with performance. We started Nonius to do that arithmetic properly, once, for everyone who needs it.

02 — Principles

Four rules we do not bend.

  1. I

    Read-only

    We ask for permission to read, never to act. If a feature would need write access to your bank, we do not build it.

  2. II

    Paid for by subscribers

    Our only income is what subscribers pay. No commissions, no payment for order flow, no ads, no data sales.

  3. III

    No advice

    We show what happened and how it was measured. What to do about it stays your decision.

  4. IV

    Measured, not predicted

    Every figure comes from your transactions and market prices by a stated method. We do not forecast returns or score your future.
