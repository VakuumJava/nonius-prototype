Time-weighted return after fees against the MSCI ACWI (net, in EUR). Both lines start at 0 % on the first day of the period, so deposits and withdrawals do not distort the comparison.

Holdings and cash on 13 Nov 2026, in EUR. Allocation describes today and does not change with the period.

Can I take these figures with me?
