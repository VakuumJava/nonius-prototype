Reset demo data

Replaces every account, transaction, alert, watchlist entry, setting and your profile on this device with the original demo data. Your colour theme stays.

Press and hold for 1.5 seconds. With the keyboard, Enter opens a confirmation dialog instead.

Delete account

Closes your Nonius account: profile, connections and all stored data are deleted, and every read-only token is revoked. Your money and accounts at banks and brokers are not affected.

Reset demo data?

Everything you changed on this device — accounts, transactions, alerts, watchlist, settings and profile — returns to the original demo data. This cannot be undone.

Delete your Nonius account?

This deletes your profile, 4 accounts and 389 transactions and revokes every connection. It cannot be undone. In this prototype it clears the demo data on this device and signs you out.
